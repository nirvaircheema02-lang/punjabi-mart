# PunjabiMarts — proposed returns and seller policy

Status: discussion draft, 28 October 2026. Not published, not enforced in checkout. India domestic launch assumption. Final legal wording and business contact details require review before launch.

## Recommended customer policy

- No change-of-mind returns for correctly supplied, accurately described products. Display the applicable policy on the product page before purchase.
- Damaged, defective, wrong, counterfeit, expired or materially misdescribed goods remain eligible for appropriate remedies. A blanket no-refund/no-return statement is not suitable. Statutory remedies and qualifying late-delivery claims are preserved.
- Offer a no-cost replacement where appropriate and acceptable to the customer. Do not force replacement or store credit where a refund is legally due. If replacement is unavailable, arrange the applicable refund.
- Ask customers to report visible transit damage/wrong items promptly, preferably within 48 hours, to help investigate. This is a reporting preference, not a hard cutoff for legal rights or hidden defects. Photos are helpful; an unboxing video is not mandatory proof.
- Optional size exchanges: defer at initial launch, unless a product explicitly offers one. Sending a size different from the ordered size remains a seller error and is not a discretionary exchange.
- Food, opened consumables, personalised and hygiene-sensitive goods: no discretionary change-of-mind return. Defect, safety, expiry and misdescription protections still apply. Never ask a customer to ship unsafe food without instructions.
- Seller/platform error: customer does not pay corrective delivery or return costs. Admin may waive physical return for unsafe or low-value goods after review.
- Cancellation before packing: existing self-service cancellation can continue. After packing, route requests to support; explain the actual fulfilment state instead of promising instant cancellation.
- Approved refunds: record amount, reason and reference; pay to the original payment method where possible. COD refunds require a verified customer-approved transfer destination, collected privately, never in public order notes. Do not mark refunded merely because a request was approved. Set a realistic processing commitment before publication; do not invent a gateway SLA.
- Provide a complaint reference, grievance contact, acknowledgement and resolution process. The 2020 Rules specify acknowledgement within 48 hours and redress within one month. Operational targets may be faster.

## Proposed seller terms

- Owner approval is required for shops and product publication. Sellers are responsible for accurate photos, descriptions, sizes, stock, authenticity, expiry and required product disclosures.
- The owner can manage platform-owned products without any seller. Their marketplace commission is zero.
- Snapshot the agreed commission when the order is placed. Later rate edits must not change historical orders.
- Calculate payable amounts from delivered, collected and eligible sales, with itemised commission and agreed adjustments. A cancelled/refunded sale must not remain fully payable.
- Hold disputed amounts while a complaint is being resolved. Record reasons and evidence, allow the seller to respond, and record the owner decision. Do not automatically blame the seller for every courier issue.
- For launch, use a manual settlement ledger with payment reference and audit trail; no paid payout integration. A configurable hold period and settlement schedule require business confirmation before publication. Settled-order refunds need explicit adjustment entries, not edited payment history.
- No automatic bank payouts, customer refund transfers or courier purchases in this phase.

## Implementation after policy agreement

Customer: order item → report issue → reason/evidence → requested remedy → reference/status.
Admin: review → request information → approve replacement/refund or reject with explanation → pickup if needed → actual replacement shipment/refund reference → close.
Seller: see own disputes, respond, and see related settlement holds/adjustments. Only admin resolves disputes and records payments.
Keep refund status, shipment status and settlement status separate. Prevent duplicate refunds, over-refunds, duplicate stock restoration and repeated payouts. Returned goods require inspection before returning to saleable stock.
